Selected publications

ConfCtrl: Enabling Precise Camera Control in Video Diffusion via Confidence-Aware Interpolation

Liudi Yang, George Eskandar, Fengyi Shen, Mohammad Altillawi, Yang Bai, Chi Zhang, Ziyuan Liu and Abhinav Valada

ECCV 2026

ConfCtrl generates video along a chosen camera path from two input views. It uses reliable 3D projections to guide the model and lets diffusion fill in uncertain regions.

CoVAR: Co-generation of Video and Action for Robotic Manipulation via Multi-Modal Diffusion

Liudi Yang, Yang Bai, George Eskandar, Fengyi Shen, Mohammad Altillawi, Dong Chen, Ziyuan Liu and Abhinav Valada

ICRA 2026

Given an instruction, an image, and the robot’s joint states, CoVAR generates a video of the task and the actions to carry it out. Bridge attention lets the video and action models exchange information during generation.

Liudi Yang*, Yang Bai* et al. · *Equal contribution

IROS 2025

Liudi Yang, Ruben Mascaro, Ignacio Alzugaray, Sai Manoj Prakhya, Marco Karrer, Ziyuan Liu and Margarita Chli

The system compares the current scan with an earlier one. When their semantic graphs match, registration supplies a constraint for correcting the robot’s trajectory.
