RoboEnvision: A Long-Horizon Video Generation Model for Multi-Task Robot Manipulation

Liudi Yang*, Yang Bai* et al. · *Equal contribution

Given a sequence of instructions, RoboEnvision generates a keyframe for each step, fills in the motion between frames, and uses the video to predict robot joint states.
